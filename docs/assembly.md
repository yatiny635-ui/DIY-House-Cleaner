# Assembly Guide

1. Cut a rigid rectangular cardboard base.
2. Mount the two drive motors on opposite sides.
3. Attach the two drive wheels.
4. Add a caster wheel under the chassis for support.
5. Build the front white collection scoop.
6. Mount the two brush motors at the front-left and front-right.
7. Secure the two circular brushes to their motor shafts/couplers.
8. Mount the Arduino UNO on the upper deck.
9. Secure the L298N and L9110S so their terminals cannot touch conductive surfaces.
10. Mount the two Witty Fox batteries securely in a suitable holder/enclosure.
11. Install the ON/OFF switch where it is easy to reach.
12. Install the 5V buck converter in a protected position.
13. Complete all wiring according to wiring.md.
14. Inspect every connection before connecting the battery.
15. Lift the robot off the floor and test the drive wheels.
16. Test each brush individually.
17. Check that both brushes sweep toward the collection scoop.
18. Place the robot on a clear floor and test at low speed.
19. Adjust brush height so the bristles contact the floor without excessive drag.
20. Secure all loose wires before normal operation.

## Mechanical tips

- Keep the chassis rigid enough that the motor mounts do not flex.
- Keep the battery low and centred where practical.
- Keep the electronics away from debris and moisture.
- The brushes should touch the floor lightly rather than being heavily compressed.
- Leave clearance around the wheels so cardboard cannot rub against the tyres.
